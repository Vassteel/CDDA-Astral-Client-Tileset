## What to write
1. **Plane facts** — one row. Type: `finite / built / claimed`. Water, weather (2–4 named weathers with their effect), peoples, cores, native portal look, and the first hour.
2. **Biome sketches** — one row per biome: foundry · markets · necropolis · hanging gardens · undercity · the walls. For each: its rule, 3–5 terrains with vanilla look-alikes, water shore, weather, **6–10 signature materials** (fantasy name · what it behaves like · dig/mine/harvest/butcher), 2–3 staple foods, a named leader, the core-seat landmark, the underground pairing.

Plane rule to honour: one walled megacity; no sky weather to speak of; curfew; tolls; one ruling core, wardens, guilds of its own.
Note: districts are the biomes; "terrains" are street/plaza/block kinds; this plane carries most of the 400-building budget, so list 10–20 building ideas per district.

## Rules
Fantasy material names; biome ids one word where possible; `?` for doubts; nothing copied from other games. This brief is the spine — the nine content tasks for Verge (items ×3, flora, creatures, POIs, extras, vanilla audit, equipment) will be cut from its ids, so make them good.
