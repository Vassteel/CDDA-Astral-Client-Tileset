## What to write
Aspect **Hearth** (Greenwood): gentle, home-making, guarding, finding the way back. Native disciplines: **Mending, Warding, Wayfinding**.

1. **T9 Spells, aspect Hearth** — ≈ 8 each in Mending, Warding and Wayfinding (tiers 0–6, at least one tier-0 hedge spell each), plus 1–2 in each of Striking, Calling, Tempering, Hexing and Shaping. Tie spells to the six biomes' rules: meadow ("nothing hunts here" — a peace-ward), drowned (water walking, breath, finding the dry way), fungal (spore-cleanse, haze-sight), root (silence for the sound rule, sinkhole sense), fallow (harvest blessing, scarecrow-calling), tallgrass (fire-break, herd-calming).
2. **Foci** — one Hearth focus line (tiers 1/2/3) for each of the three native disciplines, made from Greenwood materials (hearthwood, bog oak, ironwood, sea-silk, glowcap, heartglass…), as T1 rows.
3. **Reagents** — 4–6 Hearth reagents from Greenwood sources (T1 rows; source must exist).
4. **Grimoires** — a Hearth primer, two Hearth treatises, and 2–3 landmark "walls" (which WP-G6 landmark teaches what).
5. **Runes** — 4–6 Hearth runes (T1 + T10), each with the effect it gives when set.
6. **Casting gear** — 8–12 pieces (robes, charms, rings, staves, a lantern, a bough-crown…) as T1 + T10 rows.
7. **T10** for every magic item; **T5** for any piece that should also count as a vanilla item.
