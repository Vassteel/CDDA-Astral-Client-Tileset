## What to write
1. **Plane facts** — one row. Type: `open / natural / contested`. Water, weather (2–4 named weathers with their effect), peoples, cores, native portal look, and the first hour.
2. **Biome sketches** — one row per biome: jungle · savanna · river delta · hill forts · mangrove swamp · open-sky tropics. For each: its rule, 3–5 terrains with vanilla look-alikes, water shore, weather, **6–10 signature materials** (fantasy name · what it behaves like · dig/mine/harvest/butcher), 2–3 staple foods, a named leader, the core-seat landmark, the underground pairing.

Plane rule to honour: hot, wet; monsoon; map control shifts between visits; two or three warring peoples.
Note: name the factions, their colours and what each holds; every biome has a faction-held and a contested state.

## Rules
