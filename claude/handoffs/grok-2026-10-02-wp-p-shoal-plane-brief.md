2. **Biome sketches** — one row per biome: islands in a void, each a scrap of another plane (a meadow, a dune, a street, a glacier, a bough). For each: its rule, 3–5 terrains with vanilla look-alikes, water shore, weather, **6–10 signature materials** (fantasy name · what it behaves like · dig/mine/harvest/butcher), 2–3 staple foods, a named leader, the core-seat landmark, the underground pairing.

Plane rule to honour: tiles die over days; the void is fatal; nothing persists between visits; scavenger camps.
Note: biomes are the scrap kinds; say which other plane each borrows from and what is unique to the Shoal (void-edge materials, salvage).
