2. **Biome sketches** — one row per biome: shingle coast · wind islands · kelp shelf · inland sea · mangrove · reef wall. For each: its rule, 3–5 terrains with vanilla look-alikes, water shore, weather, **6–10 signature materials** (fantasy name · what it behaves like · dig/mine/harvest/butcher), 2–3 staple foods, a named leader, the core-seat landmark, the underground pairing.

Plane rule to honour: wet, windy; tides every 6 h expose and drown routes; wind halves ranged; lighthouse keepers; drowned towns; the Tideglass.
Note: islands are small finite maps linked by tide-paths and gates; say which biomes are island-sized.
