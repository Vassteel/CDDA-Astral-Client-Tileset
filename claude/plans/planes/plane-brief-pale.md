## Notes

- Underground pairing is the crystalline caverns: lattice-glass, mirror leather, spun glass, silver-wood, and the Lattice.
- The dead you leave are taken; do not camp beside a corpse.
- Whiteout and walking fog are the travel hazards; the valley inside the peaks is the only mild pocket.
