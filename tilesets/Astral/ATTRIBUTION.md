# Astral 0.1.0

Astral is a modified UltiCa tileset for Cataclysm: Dark Days Ahead.

UltiCa artwork and fallback configuration: the UltiCa contributors, including I-am-Erk and the artists credited in ULTICA-CREDITS.txt and the source repository: https://github.com/I-am-Erk/CDDA-Tilesets/tree/master/gfx/UltimateCataclysm . Original notices are retained.

Changes: generated Astral landscape, flora and Megafauna artwork; native sprite exports; seasonal and connected terrain mappings; atlas integration and packaging. New raster artwork was produced with OpenAI built-in image generation under user direction.
