# Megafauna artwork

46 adult and juvenile sprites generated with built-in imagegen, including the approved leaner giant horse foal. Four Defense Mode variants reuse the adults.

Loaded automatically with Megafauna when using UltiCa, ChibiUltica, or MSXotto+. Restart CDDA after installation.

Source PNGs and research notes: artifacts/megafauna-new-tileset in the CDDA workspace. Exporter: tools/megafauna-art/build_tiles.py. Native export uses binary alpha, cropping and nearest-neighbor reduction; original artwork remains unchanged.

To remove this artwork overlay, delete astra_tiles.json and sprites/astra_megafauna.png from this Megafauna folder. No monster definitions or save data are changed.
